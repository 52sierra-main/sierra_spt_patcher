# Contributing to Sierra Installer

Contributions are welcome through GitHub pull requests and other contribution
channels accepted by the project maintainer.

By intentionally submitting a contribution to the official Sierra Installer
project, you represent that you have the right to submit it and you grant Sierra
a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free license to:

- use and reproduce the contribution;
- modify it and prepare derivative works;
- include it in Sierra Installer and related Sierra projects;
- distribute it in source or binary form;
- sublicense it; and
- relicense it, including under future source-available, open-source, or
  commercial licensing terms.

This grant is intended to let the project evolve its licensing and distribution
model without requiring copyright assignment from contributors.

Submitting a contribution does **not** transfer ownership of your copyright to
Sierra.

## Licensing of submitted contributions

Unless otherwise agreed in writing before submission, contributions accepted
after the adoption of the Sierra Source-Available License 1.0 may be distributed
as part of the project under that license or any later license selected by Sierra
under the contributor grant above.

Code or other material copied from third-party projects must be compatible with
the project's licensing terms and must retain any legally required notices.

## Development forks

Public GitHub forks are permitted for non-commercial development, review, testing,
and contribution in accordance with the project LICENSE.

Please do not publish independent binary releases, mirrors, commercial versions,
or redistributed builds without prior written permission.
